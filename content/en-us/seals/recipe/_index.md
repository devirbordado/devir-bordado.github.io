title: "Recipe"
description: "Luís da Câmara Cascudo defines culinary recipes as cultural heritage, recognizing that the act of eating extends far beyond mere biological necessity. In his work 'History of Food in Brazil' (1967), he posits that a recipe serves as a living expression of a people’s memory, tradition, sociability, and identity. The recipes on this blog do not aim to teach professional gastronomy; rather, they document preparations linked to the seasons, childhood memories, and the traditional knowledge associated with specific foods. It is an effort to preserve small, everyday forms of knowledge that are typically passed down from one generation to the next."
