Knowledge is not organized into isolated boxes. At Devir Bordado, inquiry unfolds across three lines of research that do not work as separate departments but as territories that continually intertwine. They are distinct perspectives on one central question: how does making produce knowledge, memory and transformation?

Each line has its own questions, methods and fields of dialogue, but all share embroidery as language, method and conceptual operator. Below, we present these lines and the practical manifestations that emerge from each of them.
