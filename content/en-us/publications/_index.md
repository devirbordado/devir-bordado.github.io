The Publications space is dedicated to centralizing the laboratory's intellectual output. Bringing together articles, essays and theses, the environment adopts an editorial structure inspired by scientific journals to facilitate access to and retrieval of knowledge. More than an academic showcase, it is a living repository where Devir Bordado's research and its dialogues find a home and remain available for new readings.
