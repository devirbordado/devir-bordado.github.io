This work was developed within an Undergraduate Research (Iniciação Científica) project at the University of São Paulo (USP) and published in the proceedings of the USP International Symposium on Undergraduate Research and Technology (SIICUSP).

The research investigates the theoretical framework of altmetric studies in Brazilian Information Science through citation analysis. Based on articles published between 2018 and 2023 and retrieved from the SciELO, BRAPCI and Scopus databases, it identifies the authors who constitute the main theoretical reference of national scientific production on altmetrics. The results contribute to understanding the consolidation of this emerging field within Information Science and highlight the role of Brazilian researchers in its development. This record gathers the Portuguese and English versions of the work.
