Founding document of the authorial research laboratory Devir Bordado.

Devir Bordado is not born to reach a definitive form. As a research laboratory, it remains open to revising its own assumptions, to incorporating new interlocutors and to the emergence of questions that cannot yet be formulated. This document also takes part in that condition. It does not establish an immutable set of rules, but records the current state of an ongoing investigation. Its fundamental guidelines remain as a reference, while its forms may transform as the project itself matures.
