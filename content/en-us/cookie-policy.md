Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. This policy explains which ones Devir Bordado uses, what they are for and how you control each one. It complements the [Privacy policy](/en-us/privacy-policy/).

## 1. What we use

| Category | What it is for | What is stored | Duration | Needs your consent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Necessary | remember your cookie choice | `db_consent_v1`, in the browser's local storage (not a cookie and never sent to any server) | 12 months | No |
| Necessary | protect forms against bots | Cloudflare Turnstile check, loaded only when you start filling in the form | only during the check | No |
| Statistics | understand, in aggregate, how the site is used | Google Analytics: cookies `_ga` and `_ga_MRJ64TZNZ8` | up to 2 years | Yes |
| Embedded content | play videos and playlists | cookies and data from YouTube (Google) and Spotify, set by them | set by them | Yes, given by the click |

The site does not use advertising, remarketing or social network cookies. Before you choose, Google Analytics stays off: its code is not even downloaded.

## 2. How to manage your consent

- When you first enter the site, the cookie notice lets you **allow statistics** or **decline**. Both buttons have the same prominence, and declining is as easy as allowing.
- If you decline, no data is sent to Google, and any `_ga` cookies already in your browser are deleted.
- You can change your mind at any time. Use the button below or the "Manage cookies" link in the footer of any page.
- Your choice lasts 12 months and then the notice appears again. If you clear your browser data, the notice also appears again.
- You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the necessary ones may prevent forms from being sent.

## 3. Statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 only after your consent, to know which texts are read and how people reach the site. We set it up for statistics only: advertising signals, ad personalization and remarketing are disabled, and Google should not use this data for ads. The cookies `_ga` and `_ga_MRJ64TZNZ8` identify a browser and a session at random. Event data is kept for 2 months.

You can also block collection on any site with the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 4. Third-party content (YouTube and Spotify)

YouTube videos and Spotify playlists **do not load** with the page. Instead of the player you see a button. Only after you click is the player loaded from the original service, which then receives your IP address and may set cookies under its own policy. YouTube videos use the `youtube-nocookie.com` address, YouTube's enhanced privacy mode, which reduces but does not eliminate the use of cookies. If you prefer, open the content directly on the original service through the link that accompanies each player.
