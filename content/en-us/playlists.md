The Playlists space is dedicated to gathering soundtracks that accompany research and manual crafting. The selection is not random: it was chosen based on studies of how sound, rhythm, and frequency interact with the human body, influencing everything from pain perception and stress levels to attention and memory. Here, music acts as a tool for presence, a sonic territory that helps regulate the rhythm of the needle through fabric, transforming the act of embroidery into an experience of deep mindfulness and care.

Beyond that, research in neuroscience and music therapy demonstrates that music is not just sound, but an embodied experience: the tempo, intensity, and predictability of the notes directly affect cardiovascular responses, focus, and environmental perception. Between the rhythm of the music and the rhythm of the breath, the playlist becomes a space that induces calm, concentration, and endurance—all essential to the creative act.

Here, playlists become invitations to deep listening and a state of presence: the sound that cradles the thread, the silence that inhabits the stitch, and the memory revealed within the material.
