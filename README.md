## Vision

Devir Bordado aims to become a digital space dedicated to the exploration of embroidery as a form of artistic expression, research and knowledge production.

The long-term vision is to create an environment that brings together essays, research projects, visual documentation, references and creative practices, connecting textile work with memory, symbolism, nature and human experience.

---

## About the name

The name **Devir Bordado** combines two concepts related to transformation and textile practice.

**Devir** comes from the philosophical concept of becoming, referring to processes of change, movement and continuous transformation. It reflects the idea that embroidery is not only a finished object, but also a process of creation, learning and becoming.

**Bordado** refers to the traditional practice of embroidery, a technique based on threads, gestures and time. It connects the project to textile traditions, craftsmanship and the preservation of stories through material practices.

Together, the name expresses the idea of embroidery as a process of becoming, where threads, memories and meanings are continuously woven.
